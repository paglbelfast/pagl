Protestants killed during the Troubles

Remembering lives lost, with a clear explanation of the published count.

Historical figures · Checked 23 October 2026

1,288

The Sutton Index of Deaths records 1,288 people from Northern Ireland's Protestant community killed in the conflict.[1]

What the figure covers

The index's period runs from 14 July 1969 to 31 December 2001. It extends beyond the 1998 agreement.[2]

The category describes community background. It is not a count restricted to civilians, killings by one organisation or killings proven to have a sectarian motive. People from outside Northern Ireland are recorded separately in the religion summary, so this is not a worldwide total of Protestant victims.[1]

Reading the number accurately

The same table contains 3,532 deaths overall. Those figures belong to this particular index and its definitions. Community background alone does not explain an individual death or establish responsibility.[1]

Our remembrance

PAGL includes this record to acknowledge the loss experienced by Protestant families and communities. Each death represents a person and a lasting loss to those who knew them. This record does not establish a link between those deaths and Irish-language policy.

References for these figures
  1. Malcolm Sutton, Index of Deaths: religion summary, hosted by CAIN at Ulster University — Protestant category, total and classification notes.
  2. Sutton Index: updates and revisions — coverage dates.
