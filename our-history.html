Our History & Values

We are Irish Protestants and unionists, proud of our history and our place on this island. PAGL was set up because we believe our community deserves to be heard on language policy, identity and politics. You do not have to agree with us to take part.

Protestant history in Northern Ireland

Our families and communities helped build Northern Ireland: in its churches, shipyards, farms, businesses and public life. We are Irish Protestants who support Northern Ireland's place in the United Kingdom. We love the UK and believe in the rule of law. There has never been only one Protestant or unionist opinion, and we do not pretend otherwise.

Stormont, 1921–1972

The Northern Ireland Parliament operated from 1921 until its suspension in 1972. Its unionist-led governments faced sustained criticism for discrimination in housing, voting arrangements and public employment, alongside conflict over constitutional identity. Some unionists refer to this period as the “Orange State”; that phrase is politically contested. Direct rule from Westminster followed in 1972 and the old Parliament was abolished in 1973.

Concerns since the end of the old Stormont system

Many in our community feel that, since the old Stormont system ended, unionist concerns about flags, parades and cultural identity have too often been brushed aside. We want those concerns taken seriously. We also recognise that reforms were needed to tackle past discrimination. Equal rights must mean equal rights for everyone, and any claim of unfair treatment should be supported by facts.

Drumcree and parading

Drumcree is not just another headline to many unionists. It touches on faith, tradition and the freedom to parade. We believe those traditions deserve respect and a fair hearing. We also know that Garvaghy Road residents have raised serious concerns about the route. We support the rule of law and can argue for our traditions without showing hatred towards our neighbours. We will link to official decisions and report developments accurately.

For current determinations and official information, visit the Parades Commission and the Northern Ireland government information service.

Political outlook

We are unionist and centre-right. We support Nigel Farage. We supported Donald Trump in the past, but he has let us down. We judge politicians by what they do, not simply by which side they are on. These are PAGL's views; people who take part do not have to share every one of them.

Everyone is welcome to debate

PAGL is strictly non-sectarian. We welcome and support Roman Catholics as well as Protestants, and we already have a Roman Catholic member. People of every faith and none are welcome, including those who disagree with us. No sectarianism, no racism, no threats, no foul language and no personal insults. Argue your point, listen to the reply and treat people decently.

Historical discussion is presented for context. Commentary reflects attributed perspectives and should not be mistaken for findings of fact without evidence.
