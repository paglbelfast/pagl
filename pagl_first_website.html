Who we are

About PAGL

This is a first draft of the campaign's introduction. We can change the wording so it accurately reflects your aims and the people behind the project.

PAGL provides a place to discuss the impact of Gaelic-language policies on public life in Northern Ireland. We believe public policy should be open to scrutiny, public money should be accounted for, and people with different views should be able to debate the issues respectfully.

Our focus is on policies, institutions, costs and decisions—not on attacking individuals or communities because of their background.
